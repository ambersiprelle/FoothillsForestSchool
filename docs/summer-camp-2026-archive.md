## Intro

We are excited to announce Summer 2026 Explorers Camp!

Each week our Explorers camp brings a brand-new adventure! Nestled in the heart of the
Chilhowee mountains, our camp invites children to explore winding forest trails, splash
through cool mountain creeks, and discover the magic of our serene lake.

With a fresh theme every week—ranging from wilderness survival and forest art to creek
critter hunts and lake science—campers will dive into hands-on learning while connecting
deeply with the natural world. Whether hiking beneath towering pines, building shelters
from sticks and leaves, or simply watching dragonflies skim the water, every day is
filled with wonder, discovery, and fun.

Summer Camp is offered weekly — Monday through Friday, from 9AM – 2PM each day.

Let the mountains be their playground!
